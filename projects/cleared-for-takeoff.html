Cleared For Takeoff

Pre-Production

Moodboards & Style Direction

The world was designed as a lived-in, painted sci-fi environment, pulling from animated and cinematic references while keeping the family story warm and grounded.

1 / 4
World

Lived-in sci-fi

Visual inspiration included Star Wars: The Clone Wars, Dune, The Wild Robot, Robotech, The Mitchells vs. the Machines, and Arcane, with an emphasis on painted textures and weathered surfaces.

Color

Warm vs. cool

The mother was developed around warmer tones, while Leo leaned cooler. The contrast helped separate their personalities while keeping them visually connected as family.

Lighting

Cozy interiors, vast exteriors

Interior lighting was conceived as warm, ambient, and darker than the exterior, while the outdoor world leaned toward dawn and dusk naturalism.

Character Design — Leo

Leo evolved from a more formal, militaristic pilot design into a younger, more casual character. Story revisions later added his prosthetic arm and clarified the shapes for 3D modeling.

Color Exploration

Early clothing color concepts

The more regal version felt too official, so the more familiar, appealing palette became the stronger direction.

Turnaround

Design revisions for production

The first turnaround depicts an older Leo without the prosthetic arm. A later story revision made him slightly younger, added the prosthetic, changed the footwear, and simplified the line work for modeling.

Production

Shot Tracking

Keeping 113 shots organized

We needed an intuitive, simple, and organized system of tracking our 113 shots during the production process, so I created a master spreadsheet.
